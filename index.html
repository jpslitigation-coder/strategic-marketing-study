The big picture

One strategy, three moves, five sections

Strategic marketing puts the market and the customer first, then coordinates everything around them. The whole module hangs off three moves — understand the market, select a strategy, implement it — wrapped in ethics and organisational fit.

What you must be able to do

The six module learning outcomes

Every note, mnemonic and test in this dossier maps back to these. On completing the module you should be able to:

LO 1

Evaluate the challenges of developing a market-driven strategy.

LO 2

Analyse markets and develop strategic marketing segments within the competitive space.

LO 3

Demonstrate an understanding of customers, markets and strategic customer management.

LO 4

Design, implement and manage market-driven strategies.

LO 5

Assess the value of integrating ethics and corporate social investment in strategic marketing.

LO 6

Demonstrate the importance of management information systems in strategic marketing.

Where this comes from

This dossier follows the Regenesys Strategic Marketing Management study guide (2026) and its recommended texts. Always read the full guide and textbook chapters — the flashcards' page/chapter keys point you to them.

  • Hooley, Nicoulaud, Rudd, Piercy & Lee (2020) — Marketing Strategy and Competitive Positioning, 7th ed. (recommended textbook).
  • Cravens & Piercy (2013) — Strategic Marketing, 10th ed. (heavily cited framework source).
  • Walker & Mullins (2014) and Jooste et al. (2012) — segmentation, metrics and definitions.
