Responsibility is a design constraint

Do what works — and what is defensible

Profit and legality matter, but neither settles rights, fairness, vulnerability or hidden harm. Turn the decision through five tests before you commit.

Decision compassRIGHT◆ site device

Carroll’s Pyramid Carroll, 1991

PHILANTHROPICContribute voluntarily

LEGALObey binding rules
ECONOMICCreate value · stay viable (foundation)
All four at once

The four responsibilities are simultaneous expectations, not sequential stages. Philanthropy at the top cannot offset harm caused in the economic core.

The STP decision journey

Segmentation only earns its keep when it changes target choice, positioning and the marketing programme. Follow the chain; don’t stop at a demographic label.

TARGET CHOICE= segment attractiveness × competitive fit
Consumer buying

Why people choose

Need → perceive → learn → evaluate → act → experience.

Psychology, society and situation can interrupt intention — the funnel is not tidy.
B2B buying centre

The voices in the room

User · influencer · buyer · decider · gatekeeper (+ champion).

Classic five roles (Webster & Wind); translate value and risk for each. Longer, more rational cycle.
CRM & value

Keep the right customers

Engage → segment → personalise → track.

Strategy before software. CLV must net off cost-to-serve, not just revenue.
Common mistakes: a demographic label is not a segment; a large segment is not automatically attractive; a high-revenue customer is not automatically profitable.

Position first. Mix second.

A position is only credible when every element of the mix makes the same customer promise true. The 4Ps are a system, not four separate lists.

Sheet 06

Formula and exam toolkit

Print
Turn knowledge into marks

DREAM the answer

Command word first. Named theory with a purpose. Application throughout. A justified, conditional conclusion — not a summary.

Site memory device
Essay engineD-R-E-A-M◆ site device
DDefine / diagnose the focal concept and the real issue
RRelate named theory and its mechanism
EEvidence — assumptions, case facts and measures
AApply / analyse alternatives, limits and safeguards
MMake a conditional, justified judgement

Command words

Explainhow / why it works

Analyseparts + relationships

Evaluatecriteria + strengths + limits

Critically discusscompeting views + judgement

Recommendchoice + evidence + how to implement

Calculation discipline

  1. Write the formula.
  2. Substitute values with units.
  3. Show the working.
  4. Interpret the result.
  5. Challenge the assumptions.
Worked example — break-even FC R600 000 ÷ contribution R120 = 5 000 units Then ask: is demand, capacity and strategy sufficient to sell 5 000?

Distinction signals

  • Named, accurately used theory
  • Causal mechanism — not lists
  • Case facts woven throughout
  • Trade-offs and ethics addressed
  • Alternatives and conditions
  • Measures and implementation
Paragraph rhythm:Claim → theory → case evidence → implication → limitation → mini-judgement.
Redraw from memory: the DREAM chain, the five command words, and the five calculation steps — these are your under-pressure checklist.
