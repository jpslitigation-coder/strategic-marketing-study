Sheet 06

Formula and exam toolkit

Turn knowledge into marks

DREAM the answer

Command word first. Theory with purpose. Application throughout. A justified, conditional conclusion.

Essay engineD-R-E-A-M
DDefine the focal concept and issue
RRelate named theory and mechanism
EEvidence assumptions, case facts and measures
AApply alternatives, limits and safeguards
MMake a conditional judgement

Command words

Explainhow / why

Analyseparts + relationships

Evaluatecriteria + strengths + limits

Critically discusscompeting views + judgement

Recommendchoice + evidence + implementation

Calculation discipline

  1. Write the formula.
  2. Substitute values with units.
  3. Show working.
  4. Interpret the result.
  5. Challenge assumptions.
ExampleFC R600k ÷ contribution R120 = 5,000 unitsThen ask: is demand, capacity and strategy sufficient?

Distinction signals

  • Named, accurately used theory
  • Causal mechanism—not lists
  • Case facts throughout
  • Trade-offs and ethics
  • Alternatives and conditions
  • Measures and implementation
Paragraph rhythm:Claim → theory → case evidence → implication → limitation → mini-judgement.
